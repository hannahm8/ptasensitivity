




%PTA introduction
Low frequency gravitational wave (GW) detection is one of the core goals of the global pulsar timing array (PTA) effort. 
Decade-long PTA experiments are monitoring the time of arrival of an array of milli-second pulsar, searching for deviations in the pulse arrival times due to GWs~\citep{Sazhin:1978, Detweiler:1979, FosterBacker:1990}.
A prime target for PTA GW searches is the nanohertz stochastic GW background (SGWB) produced by the cosmological population of merging massive black hole binaries (MBHBs).


% about MeerKAT
The MeerKAT radio telescope is a $64$ dish interferometer located in the Karoo in the Northern Cape of South Africa.
MeerTime is the MeerKAT pulsar timing program~\citep{MeerTimeBailesEtAl:2020,MeerTimeBailesEtAl:2016}. 
MeerTime pulsar science includes study of relativistic pulsar binaries~\citep{MeerTimeRelBin:2021},
pulsar discovery in globular clusters~\citep{MeerTimeGlobularClusterPSRs:2021}, study of pulsar phenomena in the thousand pulsar array~\citep{MeerTime1000PSR:2020,MeerTime1000PSR:2021}, and the MeerTime PTA, which is the focus of this work. 


%PTA current status
PTA searches for the nano-hertz stochastic gravitational wave background are now reaching astrophysically interesting sensitivities. 
After over a decade of observations, several increasingly constraining upper limits on the SGWB amplitude have been placed by the Parkes PTA~\citep[PPTA][]{PPTAUL:2015}, European PTA~\citep[EPTA][]{EPTAUL:2015}, North American Gravitational-wave Observatory~\citep[NANOGrav][]{NANOGrav11yrUL:2018}. 
Together these three PTAs and the India PTA~\cite{JoshiEtAl:2018} form the International PTA~\citep[IPTA][]{IPTA:2016}, combining datasets to improve SGWB search sensitivity. 
Recent datasets are now showing evidence for a common noise process found by the PPTA~\cite{GoncharovEtAlPPTA:2021}, NANOGrav~\cite{NANOGrav12.5GWB:2020} and~\han{[add links to other papers when out]}.
Future observations will be used to confirm whether or not this signal the signature of a nanohertz SGWB. 
If the signal proves to be the SGWB, it will provide insights into the merging population of MBHBs~\citep{MiddEtAl:2021, CaseyClydeEtAl:2021}. 


Predicting the anticipated amplitude of the SGWB is an area of active research~\citep[see, for example][]{SykesEtAlInPrep:2021, KelleyEtAl:2017, Sesana:2013}
Equally, modelling the expected sensitivity of a PTA to a given SGWB signal aids observation planning in how to best improve sensitivity and enables time to detection forecasting.
