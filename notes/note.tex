MeerTime is the pulsar timing program of the MeerKAT radio observatory. 
One of the MeerTime projects is a pulsar timing array (PTA) campaign to search for nanohertz gravitational waves from massive black hole binaries using around $10$ years of data. 
This project is focusing on improving the PTA's signal to noise ratio (SNR) for the stochastic gravitational wave background emitted by a population of massive black hole binaries.

\section{Some notes / thoughts}
This method is definitely not finding an optimal solution, I think it finds a local maximum in SNR, but we start from a reasonable original strategy and try to improve on that, so it may not be so bad. 
At the moment, I am only changing the observation time allocation for each pulsar, so the only parameter we are changing is $\sigma_I$ which depends on $T_{{\rm obs},I}$.
We could also think about changing the cadence. 

In terms of pulsar timing data for the future, people will also be thinking about the cost of losing data for a particular pulsar.  
For example is it beneficial enough to remove a pulsar from the observation schedule and lose the opportunity for decadels-long observations and study of individual objects for the benefit of gravitational-wave detection?
