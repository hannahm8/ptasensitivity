
In this Section, we consider what S/N gains could be made if the MeerTime PTA had an additional $20\%$ observation time allcated to it. 
As an initial estimate, we increase the integration time for each pulsar by $20\%$ across the entire array (so that $T_{{\rm obs},I} \rightarrow 1.2 T_{{\rm obs},I}$). 
The corresponding S/N after $10\,{\rm yrs}$ is $13.0$, a $\approx 5\%$ increase in S/N over the current observation strategy. 

Similar to Section~\ref{sec:results}, we can also see what improvements can be made with small adjustments to the observing schedule using the time swap. 
We find similar results of a $\approx 6.6\%$ increase in S/N when using the equal-valued correlation function.
